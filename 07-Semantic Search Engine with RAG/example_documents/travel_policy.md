# Travel Policy

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business travel.

Domestic travel should use standard economy fares and reasonably priced hotels.
International travel must be approved by a manager before booking. Travelers may
claim flights, hotels, ground transportation, meals, and required visa fees when
the trip has a clear business purpose.

Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD. Expense reports should be
submitted within 14 days after the trip ends.
